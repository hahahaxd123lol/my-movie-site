FLIX2WATCH V17 — GOOGLE SEARCH / INDEXING CHECKLIST

The V17 build already contains:
- crawlable robots.txt
- sitemap.xml
- canonical URL hints
- descriptive title/description metadata
- Open Graph + Twitter social preview metadata
- WebSite + Organization JSON-LD
- dynamic Movie / TVSeries structured data on Watch pages
- a dynamic Home ItemList of movie cards
- a stable square V16 favicon/app icon family
- public title details that are visible without login
- Staff Control Center marked noindex
- Favorites marked noindex because it is account-specific

AFTER DEPLOYMENT

1. Open Google Search Console.
2. Add/verify the flix2watch.com Domain property.
3. Submit:
     https://flix2watch.com/sitemap.xml
4. Use URL Inspection on:
     https://flix2watch.com/
     https://flix2watch.com/home/
   and request indexing after the V17 deployment.
5. Inspect a few real Watch URLs after deployment to make sure Google can render
   the title, overview, poster and JSON-LD.
6. Keep the favicon URL stable after V17. Google may take days or weeks to
   recrawl and update favicon/search appearance.
7. Monitor Search Console Page Indexing, Enhancements and Performance reports.

IMPORTANT

No technical SEO change can guarantee first place, instant indexing, or any
specific Google ranking. V17 improves crawlability, canonicalization, metadata,
structured data and site identity without keyword stuffing or fake ratings.

The login wall applies only to playback. Public movie/TV details remain visible
to crawlers and visitors.
