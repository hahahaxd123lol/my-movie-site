FLIX2WATCH

Fire TV app

Watch Flix2Watch on your TV.

Install the Flix2Watch app on your Amazon Fire TV Stick. Follow the steps below — you do not need a computer or a USB cable.

Download the Fire TV app

The download button is for phones and computers. If you're installing directly on your Fire Stick, use the Downloader method below.

1

Install Downloader

On your Fire TV, open the Amazon Appstore, search for Downloader, then install and open it.

2

Allow Downloader to install apps

Open Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options → Install unknown apps, then switch Downloader on. If Developer Options is missing, open About and select your Fire TV Stick name 7 times, then go back.

3

Enter the short address

Open Downloader, select its address box and type flix2watch.com/firetv. Press Go.

4

Install Flix2Watch

When the APK finishes downloading, choose Install. When it finishes, choose Open. You can then find Flix2Watch in your Apps.
