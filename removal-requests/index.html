CONTENT & PROFILE REMOVAL

Removal Requests

Use this process to request removal or restriction of material that you believe should not appear on Flix2Watch.

What to include

Provide the exact Flix2Watch URL, identify the material clearly, explain why removal is requested, and include a reliable way for the team to contact you.

Privacy or impersonation

If a public profile, post or message exposes personal information or impersonates you, explain the specific information involved and why it should be removed.

Copyright

For copyright complaints, use the Copyright / DMCA page and provide the information needed to identify both the protected work and the allegedly infringing material.

Review process

Requests may be reviewed for authenticity, scope and abuse. Flix2Watch may remove, restrict, preserve or decline to remove material depending on the circumstances and applicable obligations.

Submit

Send removal requests through the Support page and choose the closest available concern category. Clearly put “Removal Request” in the subject.
