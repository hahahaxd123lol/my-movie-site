RIGHTSHOLDER REQUESTS

Copyright / DMCA

Flix2Watch takes credible copyright complaints seriously and provides a process for identifying material that should be reviewed.

Required information

Identify the copyrighted work, the exact Flix2Watch URL or material complained of, your contact information, and your authority to act for the rightsholder.

Good-faith statement

State that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or applicable law.

Accuracy

State that the information supplied is accurate and that you are authorised to act on behalf of the rightsholder.

Third-party players

Where a complaint concerns content hosted or controlled entirely by an external provider, Flix2Watch may direct the complaint to that provider or remove/restrict the relevant integration where appropriate.

Submit

Send the request through Support with “Copyright / DMCA” in the subject and include all relevant URLs and supporting details.
